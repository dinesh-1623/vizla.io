# Spotter Field Guide - Brief Documentation

## Vehicle Intake Form Field Descriptions

---

### **Client** *(Required)*
Identifies which client/company owns or is requesting the vehicle recovery. Determines billing, priority level, and specific handling requirements.

---

### **VIN (Vehicle Identification Number)** *(Required)*
The 17-character code that uniquely identifies the vehicle. This is the most critical field for legal verification and preventing wrong vehicle recovery.

---

### **Plate (License Plate Number)** *(Required)*
The state-issued license plate number for quick visual identification and verification by drivers before pickup.

---

### **Year** *(Required)*
The vehicle's manufacturing year. Affects vehicle value, towing method, and helps verify correct vehicle identification.

---

### **Make** *(Required)*
The vehicle manufacturer (e.g., Ford, Toyota, Honda). Determines towing equipment needs and special handling requirements.

---

### **Model** *(Required)*
The specific vehicle model (e.g., Camry, F-150, Civic). Confirms exact vehicle specification and recovery complexity.

---

### **Color** *(Required)*
The primary exterior color of the vehicle. Provides quick visual identification for drivers to locate and verify the correct vehicle.

---

### **Address** *(Required)*
The complete street address where the vehicle is located. Enables driver GPS navigation and determines zone assignment.

---

### **Reachable** *(Required)*
Indicates whether the tow truck can physically access the vehicle for immediate recovery without obstacles like gates, fences, or other vehicles blocking access.

---

### **Rusted** *(Required)*
Alerts the driver to significant rust that may affect towing safety or cause structural issues during recovery. Protects against liability for pre-existing damage.

---

### **Location Type** *(Required)*
Describes the property type (Single Family Home, Apartment Complex, Commercial, etc.) to help drivers prepare for site-specific challenges and access requirements.

---

### **Parked** *(Required)*
Describes the vehicle's parking position (Pulled In, Backed In, Parallel, etc.) to help the driver plan the towing approach and hook-up method.

---

### **Notes - Tires Deflated**
Indicates one or more tires are flat. Driver will need dollies or flatbed since the vehicle cannot be safely towed on deflated tires.

---

### **Notes - Electronic Brake**
Vehicle has electronic parking brake engaged. May require jump box or key to release, common on newer vehicles (2015+).

---

### **Notes - Mechanical Issue**
Visible mechanical problems (broken suspension, damaged steering, etc.) that may require special handling or flatbed towing.

---

### **Notes - Cosmetic Issue**
Pre-existing body damage, dents, scratches, or missing parts. Documents condition to protect company from false damage claims.

---

### **Additional Notes** *(Text Field)*
Free-form text for any information not covered above: access details, gate codes, safety concerns, special instructions, or unique circumstances.

---

### **Photos** *(Required - Max 5)*
Visual verification of vehicle identity, condition, and location. Minimum 1 photo required (VIN plate). Provides legal proof and helps drivers prepare.

**Required Photo Types:**
1. **VIN Plate** - Legal proof of vehicle identity
2. **Full Vehicle** - Overall condition and location
3. **License Plate** - Quick visual verification
4. **Damage** - Documents pre-existing damage
5. **Location/Access** - Shows obstacles or access challenges

**File Requirements:** JPG, PNG, or HEIC format, up to 10MB each

---

## Quick Reference

**Most Critical Fields:**
- VIN (must be 100% accurate)
- Address (driver depends on this)
- Photos (VIN photo required)

**Safety Fields:**
- Reachable (affects scheduling)
- Rusted (prevents equipment failure)
- Tires Deflated (requires different equipment)

**Efficiency Fields:**
- Location Type (driver preparation)
- Parked (hook-up approach)
- Notes (special instructions)
